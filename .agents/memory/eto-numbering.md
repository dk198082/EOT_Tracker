---
name: ETO numbering policy
description: Business rule for unnumbered drafts and compatibility with previously numbered drafts.
---

The submitter may create multiple drafts without consuming ETO numbers. Assign the next annual sequential ETO number only on the first successful Workflow Action “Submit to ART Review.”

**Why:** The user requested submission-order numbering, rather than creation-order numbering, so drafts can exist before entering the official review process.

**How to apply:** Identify an unnumbered draft with its internal draft label, not a placeholder ETO number. Keep the assigned number on returns to Draft and resubmissions. Allocation must commit with the successful submission, and competing or failed submissions must not duplicate numbers or consume the next number.

Previously assigned numbers, including those on historical drafts, must remain unchanged.

**Why:** Historical drafts were numbered under the previous creation-time policy. Their numbers are valid existing references, not inconsistent data to clear or renumber.

**How to apply:** Apply the unnumbered-draft rule to new drafts only; never backfill older drafts to null or restart the annual sequence.
